How CervixScan works
01

Upload a colposcopy image

Select or drag a clear cervical colposcopy photograph. The image is processed entirely on our secure server — never stored.

02

AI model inference

A TensorFlow Lite deep-learning model resizes, normalizes, and classifies the image into one of three well-defined cervical transformation zone types.

03

Review the result

Receive an instant classification with per-class confidence scores. Use the results as a clinical decision-support aid alongside professional evaluation.

Medical Disclaimer: CervixScan is intended for research and educational purposes only. It is not a certified medical device and should not replace the judgment of a qualified healthcare professional. All classifications should be reviewed and confirmed by a licensed clinician before clinical decisions are made.
